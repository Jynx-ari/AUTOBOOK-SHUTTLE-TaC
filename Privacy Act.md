# Shuttle Automation — Privacy Notice

**Since:** September 1, 2026

**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** Sept. 1, 2026
## 1. Information We Collect

The service may collect information necessary to operate Monitor and Autobook, including:

- Discord username or ID.
- Shuttle account email/username.
- Shuttle account password, when required for Autobook.
- Booking preferences.
- Booking results.
- Technical and error logs.

## 2. Why We Collect It

This information is used to:

- Operate Monitor and Autobook.
- Log into the user's shuttle account when required.
- Perform requested bookings.
- Apply the user's booking preferences.
- Send booking or monitoring notifications.
- Troubleshoot technical problems.
- Maintain service security.

We do not collect information that is unnecessary for these purposes.

## 3. Account Credentials

If Autobook requires a shuttle account password, it will be handled only for the purpose of authenticating with the shuttle service.

Passwords must not be intentionally exposed through normal bot messages, logs, or other users' interfaces.

**Credential storage:** Credentials are encrypted at rest using ** Fernet encryption**. The encryption key is stored separately from the credential data and is not stored in the Autobook source code.

## 4. Access to Information

User information is only accessible where necessary to operate, maintain, or secure the service.

Information will not intentionally be shared with other users.

We do not sell user information or use it for advertising.

## 5. Data Retention

Information will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service.

Users may request deletion of their stored information when they stop using the service.

## 6. Security

Reasonable security measures will be used to protect stored information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Your Requests

Users may contact the operator to:

- Ask what information is stored about them.
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of their information.
- Ask questions about how their information is used.
- Report a privacy or security concern.

## 8. Changes

This Privacy Notice may be updated when the service or its data practices change.

Significant changes will be communicated to affected users where appropriate.
